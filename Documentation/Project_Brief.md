# Project Brief

## Client

Global Work and Travel Ltd

## Project Type

Company Website

## Problem

The company needs a professional digital presence where customers can understand services and contact them easily.

## Proposed Solution

Develop a responsive website that presents company information, services, and contact channels.

## Target Users

- Potential travelers
- Students
- Workers seeking opportunities
- Business partners

## Success Criteria

The project should:

- Work on mobile and desktop
- Provide clear company information
- Have professional design
- Be easy to maintain
