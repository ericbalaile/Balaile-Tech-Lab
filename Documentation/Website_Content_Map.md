# Website Content Map

## 1. Purpose

The Website Content Map defines the structure, organization, and placement of content across the Global Work and Travel Ltd website.

It ensures that every page communicates the right message, supports user goals, and creates a clear journey from discovery to action.

---

# 2. Website Structure Overview

The website will contain the following main sections:

- Home
- About Us
- Services
- International Opportunities
- Travel Services
- Our Process
- Contact Us

---

# 3. Homepage Content Map

## Purpose

Introduce the company, build trust, and guide visitors toward service offerings.

## Sections

### Hero Section

Content:

- Main headline
- Supporting message
- Primary call-to-action button

Goal:

Create a strong first impression and encourage users to explore services.

---

### About Preview

Content:

- Short company introduction
- Mission overview
- Trust message

Goal:

Help visitors understand who the company is.

---

### Services Overview

Content:

- Travel solutions
- International opportunities guidance

Goal:

Show visitors the main services provided.

---

### Why Choose Us

Content:

- Professional support
- Reliable process
- Customer-focused approach

Goal:

Build confidence and trust.

---

### Call To Action

Content:

- Contact invitation
- Service inquiry guidance

Goal:

Encourage users to take the next step.

### Our Process Preview

Content:

- Step-by-step guidance
- Professional support throughout the process

Goal:

Help users understand how to engage our services.
