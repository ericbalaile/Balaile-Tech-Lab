# Website Content Map

## 1. Purpose

The Website Content Map defines the structure, organization, and placement of content across the Global Work and Travel Ltd website.

It ensures that every page communicates the right message, supports user goals, and creates a clear journey from discovery to action.

---

# 2. Website Structure Overview

The website will contain the following main sections:

- Home
- About Us
- Services
- Work Opportunities
- Travel Services
- Application Process
- Success Stories
- Contact Us

---

# 3. Homepage Content Map

## Purpose

Introduce the company, build trust, and guide visitors toward available opportunities.

## Sections

### Hero Section

Content:

- Main headline
- Supporting message
- Primary call-to-action button

Goal:

Create a strong first impression and encourage users to explore opportunities.

---

### About Preview

Content:

- Short company introduction
- Mission overview
- Trust message

Goal:

Help visitors understand who the company is.

---

### Services Overview

Content:

- Work opportunities
- Travel solutions
- Professional guidance

Goal:

Show visitors the main services provided.

---

### Why Choose Us

Content:

- Professional support
- Reliable process
- Customer-focused approach

Goal:

Build confidence and trust.

---

### Call To Action

Content:

- Contact invitation
- Application guidance

Goal:

Encourage users to take the next step.

### Application Process Preview

Content:

- Step-by-step application journey
- Required documents
- Professional support throughout the process

Goal:

Help users understand the process and feel confident starting their application.

---

### Success Stories / Testimonials

Content:

- Customer experiences
- Success journeys
- Reviews and achievements

Goal:

Build trust by showing real customer outcomes.
