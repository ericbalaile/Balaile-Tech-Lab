# Development Roadmap

## Project

Global Work and Travel Ltd Website

---

# 1. Document Overview

This document defines the development roadmap for the Global Work and Travel Ltd website project.

The roadmap follows a product engineering approach that combines business understanding, user experience design, technical implementation, quality assurance, and continuous improvement.

The goal is to build a professional, scalable, and maintainable digital platform that supports the company's work and travel services.

---

# 2. Project Execution Philosophy

The project will follow a structured product development process:

Problem Understanding

↓

Research & Discovery

↓

Product Planning

↓

UX/UI Design

↓

Development

↓

Testing

↓

Deployment

↓

Continuous Improvement

Each phase focuses on creating value for users and achieving business objectives.

# 8. Success Criteria

The project will be considered successful when:

- The website represents the company professionally.
- Users can understand services clearly.
- Visitors can easily contact the company.
- The website works across devices.
- The system is maintainable and scalable.
