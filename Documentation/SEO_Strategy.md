# SEO Strategy

## Project

Global Work and Travel Ltd Website

---

# 1. SEO Overview

This document defines the Search Engine Optimization strategy for the Global Work and Travel Ltd website.

The strategy ensures that the website can be discovered by relevant users, communicate business value clearly, build search engine trust, and support customer inquiries.

SEO activities should support both visibility and business growth.

---

# 2. SEO Goals

The primary SEO goals are:

- Increase organic visibility
- Attract relevant potential customers
- Improve website discoverability
- Build brand authority
- Support customer inquiries
- Improve long-term digital growth

SEO success should be measured by quality traffic, not only visitor numbers.

---

# 3. Target Audience Search Intent

SEO strategy should focus on understanding what users are looking for.

## Individuals

Possible search intent:

- Travel planning support
- International travel opportunities
- Visa assistance
- Holiday packages

User goal:

Find reliable guidance and travel solutions.

---

## Families

Possible search intent:

- Family travel planning
- Holiday experiences
- Travel arrangements

User goal:

Find safe and organized travel support.

---

## Business Professionals and Organizations

Possible search intent:

- Corporate travel services
- Business travel management
- Event travel coordination

User goal:

Find professional travel solutions.

---

## Secondary Keywords

# 6. On-Page SEO Guidelines

Every page should include:

## Title Tag

Should be:

- Clear
- Relevant
- Keyword focused

Example:

Travel Agency Tanzania | Global Work and Travel Ltd

---

## Meta Description

Should:

- Explain page value
- Encourage clicks
- Include relevant keywords naturally

---

## Headings

Use proper hierarchy:

- H1 for main page topic
- H2 for sections
- H3 for supporting content

---

## Content Quality

Content should be:

- Useful
- Accurate
- Customer-focused
- Easy to understand

---

Images should be optimized through:

- Alt text
- Appropriate file sizes

---

# 11. Internal Linking Strategy

Internal links should help users and search engines understand website structure.

Examples:

Homepage → Services

Services → Contact

FAQ → Application Process

Testimonials → Services

---

# 12. CTA and Conversion SEO

SEO should guide users toward meaningful actions.

Primary actions:

- Contact Us
- Request Travel Support
- Plan Your Trip
- Explore Services

CTAs should be clear and visible.

---

# 13. SEO Measurement

SEO performance should be monitored through:

- Organic traffic
- Keyword rankings
- User engagement
- Conversion inquiries
- Page performance

---

# 14. SEO URL Structure

URLs should be:

- Short
- Descriptive
- Easy to understand
- Relevant to page content

Examples:

- /services
- /visa-assistance
- /holiday-packages
- /contact

Avoid:

- Unclear URLs
- Random parameters
- Long unnecessary paths
