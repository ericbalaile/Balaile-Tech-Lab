# Feature List

## Document Status

Draft Version 0.2

This document defines website features based on current business understanding and professional travel service needs.

---

# 1. Must Have Features (MVP)

## Homepage

Purpose:

Introduce the company and communicate the value proposition of professional travel solutions.

Requirements:

- Professional hero section
- Company introduction
- Clear call-to-action
- Overview of services

---

## About Us Page

Purpose:

Build trust and credibility.

Requirements:

- Company background
- Vision and mission
- Company values

---

## Services Page

Purpose:

Explain available travel and advisory services.

Requirements:

- Service categories
- Service descriptions
- Customer benefits

---

## Contact Section

Purpose:

Allow customers to communicate with the company.

Requirements:

- Contact information
- Contact form

---

# 2. Should Have Features

## FAQ Section

Purpose:

Answer common customer questions.

Requirements:

- Service questions
- Process explanation
- Requirements overview

---

## Resources

Purpose:

Provide educational content.

Requirements:

- Travel information updates
- Professional guides

---

# 3. Could Have Features

## CRM Integration

Future possibility:

- Lead management
- Customer follow-up
- Business analytics
